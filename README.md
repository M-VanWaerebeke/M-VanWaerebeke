# Martin Van Waerebeke

I am a PhD candidate in the [ARGO team](https://www.di.ens.fr/argo/content/team.html) at Inria Paris. I study the computational possibilities and limitations of machine unlearning, and algorithms for removing the influence of training data efficiently.

[Website](https://m-vanwaerebeke.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=z_x087wAAAAJ&hl=en) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-9906-0862) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martin-van-waerebeke/)

## Research and code

| Project | Research question | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Variance-Reduced Unlearning** · ICML 2026 | How can forget-set gradients, a cornerstone of empirical unlearning, be used in certified unlearning? | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14938) · [Code](https://github.com/M-VanWaerebeke/variance-reduced-unlearning) |
| **When to Forget?** · ICML 2025 | When is unlearning computationally cheaper than retraining? | [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/van-waerebeke25a.html) |
| **SIFU** · AISTATS 2024 | Efficient client unlearning in federated optimization, with provable guarantees. | [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/fraboni24a.html) · [Code](https://github.com/M-VanWaerebeke/SIFU-AISTATS2024/tree/SIFU) |
| **Multi-class segmentation uncertainty** · UNSURE 2022 | How can uncertainty estimates account for overlap between classes? | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03587) · [Code](https://github.com/M-VanWaerebeke/Bhatta_UAMT) |

For SIFU, I contributed to the AISTATS code release and its model, dataset, and unlearning experiment support, under my former username `Mart1vw`; see the [release commit](https://github.com/M-VanWaerebeke/SIFU-AISTATS2024/commit/e940485948791cda9bc05fb4f9e199d95d3f68e4). The repository preserves its collaborative Accenture/Inria history.

I also co-authored work on [computational optical imaging](https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article/3/4/pgae164/7652001), with [code maintained by collaborators](https://github.com/carlossantosgarcia/PnP_Lantern).
